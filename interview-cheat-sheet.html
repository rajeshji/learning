Senior Interview Answer — Say This First

"I think in layers: load, infra, dependency, database, consistency, coordination. Then I apply mitigation buckets: absorb, reduce, isolate, recover, preserve correctness."

This one sentence signals Principal-level thinking. Say it before diving into specific failures.

The Universal Failure Chain — Know This Cold

Traffic spike / node failure / slow dependency
   ↓
Thread pool / connection pool exhaustion
   ↓
Timeouts → client retries → retry storm
   ↓
Backend overload → more failures → cascading
   ↓
Data inconsistency (partial writes, duplicates, stale reads)
   ↓
Replication lag → split brain → coordination failure
   ↓
Full outage

Break the chain at ANY step with: circuit breaker · bulkhead · idempotency · quorum
